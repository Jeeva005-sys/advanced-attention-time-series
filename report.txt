Advanced Attention-based Time Series Forecasting

1. Baseline Model:
We implemented a baseline ARIMA-style approach using classical time series modeling for comparison.

2. Attention Model Architecture:
An Encoder-Decoder LSTM with Attention mechanism was implemented. Encoder captures temporal dependencies while Attention dynamically weighs important time steps.

3. Hyperparameter Search:
Grid search over:
- Sequence length: 10, 20
- LSTM units: 32, 64
- Train split: 70%, 80%

4. Metrics:
MAE, RMSE, and MAPE were computed for each configuration.

5. Interpretability:
Attention weights indicate which historical time steps contributed most to predictions, improving explainability over standard LSTM.

6. Results:
Results are recorded in results.csv with full grid combinations.

7. Conclusion:
Attention-based models outperform baseline in accuracy and interpretability.
